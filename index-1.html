VYROVA

PHOTO STUDIO · PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how VYROVA Photo Studio handles information when you use the app. VYROVA is an image-editing app.

1. Photos and editing

Photos you choose from your device or capture with the camera are used to provide editing features. Editing, filters, adjustments and export are performed locally in the app. VYROVA does not operate a photo-upload account or its own cloud photo storage service. Photos are not sent to VYROVA servers by the editing features described here. When you choose Share, Android passes the image to the app or service you select; that recipient’s privacy policy then applies.

2. AI Background Removal

AI subject/background removal uses Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation. The model may be downloaded to your device through Google Play services. Image segmentation runs on the device; the selected photo is processed locally by this feature and is not uploaded to VYROVA for AI processing. Google Play services may handle model delivery according to Google’s terms and privacy information.

3. Camera and photo access

Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. Photo access is used when you choose an image to edit or save an export. You can manage app permissions in Android Settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work.

4. Advertising and third-party services

VYROVA uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including rewarded ads. Google’s SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), advertising or app/device identifiers, ad views and taps or other app interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Google and its advertising partners may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact data handled can depend on SDK versions, settings, region and the ad features in use. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Internet and model downloads

An internet connection is used for ad services and to obtain the AI subject-segmentation model when needed. The model is delivered by Google Play services. VYROVA does not use the internet connection to upload your edited photos to a VYROVA photo account.

6. Storage, retention and security

Exports are saved on your device in the location selected or provided by Android. VYROVA may keep a small local list of recent edit references and temporary files needed for editing, exporting or sharing. You can remove saved exports using your device’s file or gallery apps; clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes app-private local data. VYROVA does not maintain a VYROVA cloud account or server-side photo library. No app can guarantee absolute security; keep your device and Android software updated.

7. Children

VYROVA is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to VYROVA or through an ad service, contact the developer using the support contact on the app’s official store listing.

8. Your choices

You can choose which photos to open, whether to use the camera, whether to watch an optional rewarded ad, and whether to share an export. You can manage camera and photo permissions in Android Settings and manage advertising preferences through the controls available from Google and your device. Availability of ad choices may vary by location.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The latest in-app version will show its update date. For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on VYROVA’s official Google Play listing.

Developer release checklist: Before public release, publish this policy on a publicly accessible HTTPS webpage, add that URL to the Google Play listing, and ensure the Play Console Data safety form matches the app and all SDK data practices.
