PHOTO STUDIO · PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how VYROVA Photo Studio handles information when you use the app.

1. Photos and Editing

Photos selected from your device or captured with the camera are used to provide editing features. Editing, filters, adjustments and export are performed locally in the app. VYROVA does not operate its own cloud photo storage service. When you share an image, the privacy policy of the recipient service applies.

2. AI Background Removal

AI subject and background removal uses Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation. The model may be downloaded through Google Play services. Image segmentation runs on your device and selected photos are not uploaded to VYROVA servers for AI processing.

3. Camera and Photo Access

Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. Photo access is used when you select an image to edit or save an export. You can manage permissions through Android Settings.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

VYROVA uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including rewarded advertisements. Google’s SDK may collect information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Internet and Model Downloads

An internet connection is used for advertising services and downloading the AI subject-segmentation model when needed. VYROVA does not use its own servers to upload edited photos.

6. Storage, Retention and Security

Exports are saved on your device. VYROVA may keep local editing references and temporary files needed for editing and exporting. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes app-private local data. VYROVA does not maintain a cloud photo library.

7. Children

VYROVA is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. For privacy questions, use the developer support contact provided on the official app store listing.

8. Your Choices

You can choose which photos to edit, whether to use the camera, whether to watch optional rewarded advertisements and whether to share an export. You can manage app permissions through Android Settings and advertising preferences through available Google controls.

9. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact on VYROVA’s official Google Play listing.
